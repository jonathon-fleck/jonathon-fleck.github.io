Jonathon Fleck

I am a sixth year PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Utah and I am working with Aaron Bertram. Before moving to Utah, I received my B.S. and M.S. at Michigan State University.

I am interested in algebraic geometry. Recently, I have been focused on applying representation theory and combinatorial structures to study stability conditions on the derived category of coherent sheaves on a projective variety.
